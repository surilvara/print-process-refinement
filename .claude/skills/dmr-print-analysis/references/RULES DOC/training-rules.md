GENERAL GUIDELINE: COSTUME JEWELLERY vs JEWELLERY
COSTUME JEWELLERY

Are considered part of this category all items made of:
Metals such as brass, bronze, copper, stainless steel, aluminum.
Non-metallic materials such as glass, plastic, resin, wood, shells, fabric, ceramic, craft foam.
Artificial stones or decorative crystals.
All product lines such as EARRINGS, BRACELETS, RINGS, NECKLACES, TIARAS, BROOCHES, CHARMS should be recorded under COSTUME JEWELLERY.
➤ GOLD PLATED does not refer to precious metal, but it is usually a base of metal (like brass or copper) covered with a thin layer of gold. For this reason, it should be categorized as COSTUME JEWELLERY.
NOTE: The price is not an element to be taken into consideration to understand if an object is costume jewellery or jewel; it is only indicative, in fact, costume jewellery from brands like Chanel, Louis Vuitton and similar has even higher prices than jewels from other brands.
JEWELLERY

Are considered part of this category all the items made of :
Gold, common metal (such as steel, bronze, and similar), and silver with  semi-precious stones like zircons, quartz , aquamarines, amethysts, topazes, hard stones (corals, turquoises, jades, amber) silver, platinum, palladium.
Precious stones like diamonds, rubies, sapphires; semi-precious gems.
Natural pearls, synthetic diamonds are normally considered part of this category.

Are part of this category the following product line:  EARRINGS,  RINGS, BRACELETS, NECKLACES.
Each product line is used for the corresponding item.

«JEWELLERY OTHER» is used for broches, tiaras, charms and everything that is not linked to a specific product line.

OLD RULE: HIGH JEWELLERY vs JEWELLERY
JEWELLERY

Are considered part of this category all items made of gold or silver with gems like zirconium, aquamarines, amethyst, topaz, coral, jade and pearls.
In this category there are some of the following product lines: «NECKLACE», «RINGS», «BRACELETS», «JEWELS OTHERS».
Each product line is used for the relative item.
«JEWELS OTHERS» » is used for broches, tiaras, charms and everything that is not attributable to a specific product line.

FINE (HIGH) JEWELLERY

Are considered part of this category all the items made of platinum and the ones made of gold or silver if and only if they have gemstones like diamonds, emeralds, rubies, sapphires.
In this category there are some of the following product lines: «FINE JEWELLERY NECKLACE», «FINE JEWELLERY EARRINGS», «FINE JEWELLERY RINGS», «FINE JEWELLERY BRACELETS», «FINE JEWELLERY OTHER».
Each product line is used for the relative item.
«FINE JEWELLERY OTHER» is used for broches, tiaras, charms and everything that is not attributable to a specific product line.
NOTE: Brands like Morellato, Molecole, Bliss, Dodo and such, do not sell Fine Jewellery.

### Notes:

NEW RULE: HIGH JEWELLERY vs JEWELLERY
Everything presented as belonging to JEWELS category will be recorded as JEWELSOnly items specifically mentioned as HIGH JEWELLERY will be recorded as HIGH JEWELLERYThe classification is NO MORE BASED ON the MATERIALS but on HOW THE PRODUCT IS PRESENTED

![](GoogleShape370p62.jpg)
EASIER AND QUICKER DECISION ON HOW TO RECORD DATA
MORE CONSISTENCY AMONG DATA
MORE ALIGNMENT WITH THE POINT OF VIEW OF THE READER
At this moment the p.lines related to High Jewellery are wrongly named as FINE JEWELLERY; they will be renamed as HIGH JEWELLERY as soon as possible:
Earrings Fine Jewellery —> Earrings High Jewellery
Rings Fine Jewellery —> Rings High Jewellery
Bracelets Fine Jewellery —> Bracelets High Jewellery
Necklaces Fine Jewellery —> Necklaces High Jewellery
Fine Jewellery Other —> High Jewellery Other(used for brooches, tiaras, pendants, charms, and anything else that is not traceable to a specific underline)
FINE ≠ HIGH JEWELLERYFINE JEWELLERY SHOULD BE RECORDED AS JEWELLERY

## 10. Collaboration Brands [Updated: 2025-01]

COLLABORATION BRANDSStarting from Jan ‘25 data we will apply a new rule to evaluate collaboration brands:- No brands for the collaboration (i.e. ARMANI/DADA, OMEGA X SWATCH etc)  will be created anymore and we will progressively deactivate the old ones still active in order to improve the consistency of our data- Basically all brands taking part at the collaboration will be evaluated for the space occupied by the text and/or image referring to the collaboration itself —> this means that the space will be doubled not only for brands belonging to different sectors but also for the ones belonging to the same sector
- In case the collaboration is between a brand and the designer/stylist of another brand (i.e. Paco Rabanne x H&M, Stella McCartney x Adidas etc) , only the real brand producing the collection will be evaluated for the space occupied while the one of the designer/stylist will be recorded as one mention - In Adv pages, the two brands that collaborate will split the space

# Beauty industry - starting point
Second industry after fashion in terms of delivered data to LVMH group
Average of 47,94% of records with product association in S2 2023
43,88% of all active products in the database belonging to beauty industry

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 5 -->
# What did we do?
Analyzed the taxonomy of 61 brands as first priority batch, divided in 3 sectors: fragrances, make-up, skincare.
Checked the websites of each brand to compare with the products already present in the database
Presented the new taxonomy and after validation from business team, worked on the products database creating new products, hiding old ones and fixed typos.
Project started late November 2023 and lasted until mid-April 2024.
Around 700 new products were created in WD between February and April

# First results
52,35% on average in the Q1 2024 of records with product association (already a 8,63% increase from Q4 2023)
Some brands have a totally new product taxonomy (formerly with almost no products on the database or with very old products), i.e. Tata Harper, Too Faced, SK-II.
Old products no longer sold and old products related to collections from up until 10 years ago were hidden to production, making the search for products during the data entry process easier.

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 10 -->
# What comes next
We expect an even higher increase in the % of records with product association due to the massive amount of new products in the database.
At the same time we expect to see less errors related to products, both in terms of missing info and inaccurate information (wrong products allocated to a record).
KPIs related to % of records with product association and errors are regularly shared with LVMH team and focused checks on product association will be carried out for beauty.

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 11 -->
# Key points to remember
Pay extra attention to the brands shared in this deck, as they are top priority ones. Keep in mind that it’s more likely to find a product in the database for these brands than not, so be sure to check thoroughly before leaving the field blank!
Beauty products are always linked to a s-line. If you don’t find the product after selecting the s-line, try insert the product first. This applies especially for skincare vs. makeup products and for unisex/women’s/men’s fragrances which are often are the trickiest ones.
If the product description/what’s on the package doesn’t match exactly the product name, but you can identify the generic product, use the generic product whenever possible rather than leaving the field blank!
This is an ongoing project, new brands will be analyzed and will have a new product taxonomy during the year. While we ask you to pay specific attention to the brands mentioned here, check for product as usual for the other brands!
