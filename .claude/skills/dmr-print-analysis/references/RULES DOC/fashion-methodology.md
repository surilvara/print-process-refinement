FASHION ON COVER – GATEFOLD
When a cover is developed as an editorial Gatefold, all pages are registered with position «OUTSIDE FRONT COVER" indicating that it is a Gatefold.

We give as many Full Pages as the amount of pages that form the Gatefold (in the examples 3 Full Pages)

In case of a single shot gatefold (i.e. Vanity Fair), since it would be impossible to give 3 FP in one single record, we will record the brands  of each page alone (using the code ‘image 2’ to show the image of following page).

In case of a gatefold with more than one shot (i.e. Glamour), the first is inserted by itself while the second page is inserted as a Double Page.

ADDRESSES –  PREVIEWS – OPENINGS
Often in the address, preview and opening pages there are photos of the products.

If the photo is captioned it is evaluated for the space occupied (regardless of whether it's a photo of the product or if it is credit on model).

In case there is no description:
If the product is recognizable from the photo, it is assigned the space occupied
If the photo is recalled from an article inside the magazine, it is assigned a PICTURE

If the photographed product is recognizable because you see the brand clearly written on the item, it is evaluated by the space occupied, regardless of the presence of a caption.

MONOGRAPHY
We define monographies as articles, columns and features entirely dedicated to one or more brands of the same Company that may cover one or more pages, except for newspapers that can occupy even less than an entire page.

PRODUCT MONOGRAPHIC ARTICLES
The monographic article is focused on one product and one or more product lines, often accompanied by text. The value to be assigned will be the total space that also includes the text part. Regardless if it’s a worn article or a still life, the whole space can be attributed to the product/product line and any references to other products of the same brand are omitted.
ATTENTION: articles about the launch of NEW PRODUCTS should normally be recorded as PRODUCT MONOGRAPHY even if structured as event or interview

CORPORATE MONOGRAPHIC ARTICLES
Articles about the institutional sphere of the company/brand: events, sponsorships, interviews, etc.
Usually, the space is entirely attributed to the corporate category unless there is a specific product, photographed and described. In this case, the space occupied by the product is considered separated from the space relative to the institutional sphere.
ATTENTION: IN CASE OF PRODUCTS CREATED IN COLLABORATION WITH AN ARTIST, THE SPACE SHOULD BE DIVIDED BY PRODUCT AND CORPORATE ACCORDING TO THE SPACE OCCUPIED (the most of times the space should be equally divided 50% to corporate and 50% to product)

TESTIMONIAL MONOGRAPHIC ARTICLES
In an article about a testimonial with a space dedicated to the product that he/she is representing, we evaluate it separately from the text.

 there have to be the pictures of the advertising campaign that the subject did for the brand and the relationship between the brand and the celebrity has to be clearly specified.

 there has to be an unquestionable reference to the fact that the story was organized in cooperation with the press office of the brand to whom the celebrity is connected.

initiative sponsored by a brand for a cultural and/or sporting effort, from which the brand itself benefits from an image standpoint

PROD LINES
FINE JEWELLERY
Are considered part of this category all the items made of platinum and the ones made of gold or silver if and only if they have gemstones like diamonds, emeralds, rubies, sapphires.
In this category there are some of the following product lines: «FINE JEWELLERY NECKLACE», «FINE JEWELLERY EARRINGS», «FINE JEWELLERY RINGS», «FINE JEWELLERY BRACELETS», «FINE JEWELLERY OTHER».
Each product line is used for the relative item.
«FINE JEWELLERY OTHER» is used for broches, tiaras, charms and everything that is not attributable to a specific product line.
NOTE: Brands like Morellato, Molecole, Bliss, Dodo and such, do not sell Fine Jewellery.
JEWELLERY

Are considered part of this category all items made of gold or silver with gems like zirconium, aquamarines, amethyst, topaz, coral, jade and pearls.

In this category there are some of the following product lines: «NECKLACE», «RINGS», «BRACELETS», «JEWELS OTHERS».

Each product line is used for the relative item.

«JEWELS OTHERS» » is used for broches, tiaras, charms and everything that is not attributable to a specific product line.

NOTE: All jewellery with no gemstones (of brands that do not have costume jewellery) in silver or metal (steel, bronze or such), or synthetic pearls is registered under the product line attributable to «jewellery» (rings, earrings, necklaces, bracelets, other) associating the product «Silver/Metal/Acrylic Beads»
